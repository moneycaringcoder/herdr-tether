# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Criticise code, not
the person who wrote it, and remember that the person on the other side of a
review is spending their own time here.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

This project is maintained by one person, who is responsible for clarifying and
enforcing these standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action
in response to any behaviour deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful.

The maintainer has the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits,
code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this code of
conduct, and will explain the reasons for moderation decisions when
appropriate.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@moneycaringcoder](https://github.com/moneycaringcoder),
through GitHub — either by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/moneycaringcoder/herdr-tether/security/advisories/new),
which is private and does not have to concern a vulnerability, or by contacting
them through their GitHub profile.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.
